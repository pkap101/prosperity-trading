# IMC 3 (Last Year) Product Analysis & Comparison to IMC 4

## Purpose
Analyze each IMC 3 product based solely on news perception, compare to IMC 4 products where parallels exist, then see how actual results differed from initial perception.

## IMC 3 Product Analysis

### 1. Quantum Coffee

**News Summary:**
- Promised to be "energy booster of the future"
- Users reported sleepless nights, uncontrolled jitters
- Doctors assessed long-term health effects
- Preliminary report reveals "dangerously high levels of Quantum Jitterwaves"
- Symptoms: extreme restlessness, short-term memory glitches, severe smelly coffee breath
- **Authorities debating an IMMEDIATE BAN**

**Initial Perception:**
- Team 1: Strong Sell, 25% allocation (health/regulatory overhang, top alpha driver)
- Team 2: `---` (-40% expected)
- Consensus: Very bearish, clear crisis signal

**News Quality Assessment:**
- Multiple concrete negatives: health report, symptoms listed, ban being debated
- "Immediate ban" is severe regulatory action
- No positive counterbalance in the article
- Very similar structure to a product recall/health crisis

**IMC 4 Comparison: LAVA CAKE**
| Aspect | Quantum Coffee (IMC 3) | Lava Cake (IMC 4) |
|--------|------------------------|-------------------|
| Core issue | Health effects discovered | Actual lava found in product |
| Regulatory action | Ban being debated | Sales halted, formal review |
| Legal exposure | Not mentioned | Lawsuits "piling up" |
| Vendor response | Not mentioned | Returning stock with lawyers |
| Severity | High | High (arguably more concrete) |

*Assessment:* Lava Cake has MORE concrete negative catalysts (sales already halted vs. ban "being debated", plus lawsuits). If Quantum Coffee moved -67%, Lava Cake could move similarly or more.

**Actual Result:** -66.79% (exceeded -50% expectation, matched Team 2's -40% direction)

**Initial Perception:**

**News Quality Assessment:**

**IMC 4 Comparison: PYROFLEX CELLS**
| Aspect | Solar Panels (IMC 3) | Pyroflex Cells (IMC 4) |
|--------|----------------------|------------------------|

### 3. Cacti Needle (Rail Spikes)

**News Quality Assessment:**

## Lessons for IMC 4 Allocations

1. **Health/regulatory crises (Quantum Coffee → Lava Cake):** Actual moves can exceed expectations. -67% happened. Don't undersize bearish bets on clear crises.

2. **Tax policy shocks (Solar Panels → Pyroflex Cells):** May underperform. Solar Panels only moved -9% despite "triples the cost." Consider reducing Pyroflex allocation.

3. **Product blame incidents (Cacti Needle → Obsidian Cutlery):** Can move significantly (-41%). Worth betting on.

4. **User growth metrics (VR Monocle → Thermalite Core):** Reliable signal. +22% validated the thesis. Thermalite Core should be sized appropriately.

5. **M&A (Ranch Sauce → Magma Ink):** May not move if deal is priced in. Ranch Sauce was flat. Magma Ink has more demand evidence though.

6. **Hype without substance (Moonshine, Striped Shirts → Volcanic Incense, Scoria Paste):** Skip is correct. Both moved <1%.

7. **Supply destruction (Red Flags → ???):** No IMC 4 parallel. Key lesson: scarcity can be bullish, not bearish. We have no supply destruction story this year.

8. **Skipping uncertain signals:** Team 2's skip decisions (x) were validated for Moonshine, Striped Shirts. Our skips on Volcanic Incense and Scoria Paste are likely correct.

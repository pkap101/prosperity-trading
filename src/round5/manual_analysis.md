## Product-by-Product Analysis

### 1. Thermalite Core

**Direction:** BUY

**Expected Movement:** +20%
**Expected Range:** +12% to +30%

**News Summary:**
- Quarterly forecast shows sharp increase in Thermalite-powered smart home devices
- Active projected users: 1.42M → 3.89M (+174% growth)
- Average net activity time: 16 hours 42 minutes/day (sustained use)
- Analysts speculate "very strong next quarter"

**Analysis:**
- Quantitative data provided (rare in these articles) - makes signal very reliable
- +174% user growth is massive; even partial translation to price = large move
- Forward-looking projections suggest sustained momentum, not one-time spike
- "Sustained household use" indicates structural demand, not fad
- Upside skewed: growth could exceed projections; downside limited by real demand

**Risk Factors:**
- Projections are forecasts, not actuals
- Smart home sector could face macro headwinds

### Key Lessons That May Influence Our Allocations

1. **Actual moves ranged -66.79% to +50.90%**
   - The game allows for very large price swings
   - Our ranges may be reasonable, but tails could be fatter than expected

2. **Crisis signals can exceed expectations**
   - Quantum Coffee moved -67% vs -50% expected
   - *Implication:* If Lava Cake is truly a crisis, -25% might be conservative; the -35% end of our range is plausible

3. **Skip decisions were validated**
   - Moonshine (+3%) and Striped shirts (+0.21%) had minimal movement
   - *Implication:* Skipping Volcanic Incense and Scoria Paste is likely correct

4. **Hedging/reducing positions was costly**
   - They left ~70k on the table vs optimal
   - *Implication:* If we're confident in a signal, don't under-allocate

5. **Compromising on weak/debated signals led to losses**
   - Ranch Sauce debate → compromise → loss
   - *Implication:* Better to skip uncertain products than half-commit

6. **Some bearish signals underperformed (Solar Panels -30% expected, -9% actual)**
   - *Implication:* Pyroflex Cells (tax policy) could underperform our -12% estimate

7. **Clear bullish signals were underestimated (Red Flags +5% expected, +51% actual)**
   - *Implication:* If Thermalite Core signal is truly strong, +30% upside is possible

### How This Could Influence Our Allocations

| Consideration | Potential Adjustment |
|---------------|---------------------|
| Crisis signals overshoot | Could increase Lava Cake allocation slightly |
| Skip decisions validated | Confident in skipping Volcanic Incense, Scoria Paste |
| Hedging was costly | Don't reduce allocations on high-conviction trades |
| Some bearish underperforms | Maybe slight caution on Pyroflex Cells |
| Clear bullish underestimated | Could increase Thermalite Core allocation slightly |
| Wrong direction possible | Keep allocations sized so errors don't wipe out gains |

**Overall takeaway:** The historical data suggests being more aggressive on high-conviction trades while maintaining discipline on skips. However, we should base our actual percentage estimates on our news analysis, not on mapping to last year's products.
